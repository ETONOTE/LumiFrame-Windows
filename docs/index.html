How to upscale anime on Android

Open a local video in LumiFrame and choose Anime4K to upscale it as it plays. Pause on a frame with fine line art, switch to V3, then replay the same scene to compare the outlines and motion on your phone.

Save an upscaled anime video as MP4

For a file you can watch in another player, open video conversion, choose the source clip and a model, then save the MP4. Real-time playback is also available when you just want to watch the original clip.
